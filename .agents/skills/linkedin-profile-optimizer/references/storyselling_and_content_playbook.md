# Brand StorySelling & Content Playbook Reference

## 1. The 10 Foundational Positioning Questions
Before rewriting a profile, map the user's background to these 10 anchors:
1. **Anchor Word/Phrase:** What is the core phrase people should associate with you? (e.g., *Consumer, Product, AI & Data Transformation*)
2. **Target Audience:** Who are you speaking to? (e.g., *PE Operating Partners, Chairs, Board Search Partners* vs. *B2B Buyers* vs. *Recruiters*)
3. **Core Value Delivered:** What is the single most valuable bridge you build for them?
4. **Hard Numbers & Receipts:** P&L sizes, revenue scale, MBO/transformation outcomes, awards, and iconic products built.
5. **Contrarian Point of View:** What do you believe about your industry that most people get wrong?
6. **Human Personality:** How do you actually show up in the room? (Energy, coaching ethos, background, human quirk)
7. **Core 5–10 Skills:** Search-matched skill clusters for your target niche.
8. **Ideal Inbound Message:** Who do you want reaching out and for what exact mandate?
9. **Flagship Proof Asset:** The single best perspective piece, case study, or transformation story for your Featured section.
10. **Primary Next Step (CTA):** What should a visitor do after reading your profile?

## 2. The 5-Paragraph Brand StorySelling "About" Framework
Every great About section uses a mini brand-story arc (**Hero** = your audience/board/client; **Villain** = the industry disconnect or problem they face; **Transformation** = how your unique combination of skills solves it):

1. **Paragraph 1 — The 265-Character Hook & UVP:** State clearly who you help, across what domains, and at what scale so LinkedIn's AI summary and human skimmers immediately grasp your value before clicking *"See more"*.
2. **Paragraph 2 — The Contrarian Point of View:** Name the broken status quo (e.g., treating AI/technology and physical/digital consumer product as separate silos) and state your belief on how real value is unlocked.
3. **Paragraph 3 — The 3 Proof Pillars (Receipts):** Group career achievements into 3 scannable bullet pillars with hard numbers (£/$/% scale, iconic brands, physical/digital products launched, board/PE mandates).
4. **Paragraph 4 — Personality & Leadership Culture:** Share how you operate with teams, your coaching/leadership philosophy, and a specific human detail that makes you *"one in a million"* rather than *"one of a million"*.
5. **Paragraph 5 — The Clear CTA:** State explicitly what conversations or mandates you welcome next.

## 3. Ongoing Engagement & Content Systems (Two Modes)

### Mode A: Executive / Boardroom Low-Lift Routine (15–30 Mins/Week)
- **Dream 50/100 Engagement First:** Curate 50–100 target peers, Chairs, PE Partners, and CEOs. Leave 2–3 substantive, 3-sentence operator insights on their posts each week so your optimized Headline appears in front of their entire audience.
- **1–2 High-Value POV Posts/Month:** Share personal stories + boardroom lessons or contrarian takes rooted in lived operator/board experience.

### Mode B: Full Creator Engine (4 Posts/Week + Daily Engagement)
- **Tuesday (Educate / Authority):** Solve one specific ICP problem using PAS (*Problem → Agitate → Solution*) or an 8–12 slide PDF carousel (renamed with SEO keywords before upload).
- **Wednesday (Document / Proof):** Share behind-the-scenes lessons, real metrics, or frameworks in action (*"Show, don't tell"*).
- **Thursday (Storytell / Growth):** Use SLAY (*Story → Lesson → Actionable Advice → You/Question*).
- **Sunday (Convert):** Share a transformation breakdown or case study pointing to your Featured asset or newsletter.
- **The "3+1" Post-Publish Rule:** Within 60 minutes of posting, add 3 self-comments under your post (1. context/photo, 2. extra insight, 3. link to resource—keeping external links out of the main post body).
