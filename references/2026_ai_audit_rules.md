# 2026 LinkedIn AI Ranking & Audit Reference

## 1. How the 2026 Unified AI Algorithm Reads a Profile

LinkedIn evaluates **Profile + Content + Engagement** together via a unified LLM ranking architecture:

- **The Profile is an AI Prompt:** LinkedIn reads profile fields as context to route posts and rank candidates in Recruiter and external LLM answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI).
- **The 5 Core Author Data Fields** (travel with every post and comment):
  1. **Name** (Clean, real, searchable)
  2. **Headline** (Highest algorithmic and human leverage—220 chars max)
  3. **Company** (Must link to an official, active Company Page)
  4. **Industry** (Must align with target search filters, e.g., *Venture Capital & Private Equity*, *Executive Offices*, *Retail*, or *Business Consulting*)
  5. **Title** (Must align with target buyer/recruiter search strings)
- **Secondary Search & AI Summary Signals:**
  - The **first 265–300 characters of the About section** (before the *"See more"* fold) are heavily weighted by LinkedIn Recruiter's AI summary generator.
  - **Top 3–5 Pinned Skills** and **Skill Clusters** attached to specific Experience entries determine semantic search ranking.

## 2. 5-Pillar Scoring Rubric (1–10 Scale)

| Pillar | Weight | 9–10 Benchmark | 1–4 Red Flags |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Algorithmic Alignment** | 25% | Headline, Industry, Title, About, and Top 5 Pinned Skills all reinforce one cohesive niche and target persona. | Headline is just a title list; legacy/tactical skills (`Direct Marketing`) pinned at the top of a Board/C-suite profile; unlinked company pages. |
| **2. Above-the-Fold Clarity (10-Sec Test)** | 25% | Banner + Photo + Headline + Contact/Custom Button immediately answer *what you do*, *who you help*, and *why trust you*. | Generic banner; title-only headline; outdated former employer URL in Contact Info; missing scale proof. |
| **3. About Section & AI Hook** | 20% | First 265 chars state the exact UVP/outcome; follows the 5-paragraph StorySelling arc (Hook → Contrarian POV → Proof → Personality → CTA) in first person. | Opens with generic resume history; third-person voice; AI buzzwords; buries key differentiators. |
| **4. Proof, Experience & Hygiene** | 15% | Context + quantified CAR (Challenge → Action → Result) bullets; product/commercial wins explicitly surfaced inside each role; zero typos. | Committee/task lists without outcomes; major career achievements mentioned in Summary but missing from Experience roles; typos or duplicate role entries. |
| **5. Conversion & Reference Architecture** | 15% | Curated Featured section (2–3 items); active Services categories; 3–5 targeted Recommendations acting as a third-party reference check. | Empty or stale Featured section; no clear CTA; zero recent Recommendations. |
